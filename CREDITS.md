## Default avatar · Avatar por defecto

`assets/img/nostrich.png` — **Purple Nostrich Icon** by **SovrynMatt**, cropped to a round 256 px avatar · recortado como avatar redondo de 256 px.
Source · Fuente: <https://github.com/SovrynMatt/Nostr-Website-Button-Design>

The author releases it as free and open source and invites people to use it on their websites.
El autor lo publica como software libre y de código abierto e invita a usarlo en webs.

If the file is missing, Mado falls back to a small ostrich drawn in its own code.
